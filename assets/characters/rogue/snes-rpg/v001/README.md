# Rogue board sprite

Rogue is distinct from Commander. Preserve short dark hair, aviator sunglasses, ivory coat with burgundy lining, red scarf, charcoal shirt, olive trousers, brown gloves/boots, straps and pouches. No sword or staff. Original Office pointing/breathing/glasses-glow and Blender sources remain untouched.

Built-in imagegen source and exact prompt are retained. Reproduce with animation/package_rogue_snes_rpg_v001.py using Pillow/numpy. Complete connected poses are sorted into 33-pose layout, registered by head and foot baseline, sampled directly to 64px runtime and 32px compatibility with a shared 24-color palette and binary alpha. Display scale 1.2 and foot origin 62/64 match the other converted characters.

Idle breathing and scarf motion, four-phase walking, pointing with cyan sunglasses glint, and hurt reactions. Left mirrors right. Point timeline 180/220/1300/180/140ms for poses 0/1/2/1/0. Focus, work/review and occasional idle trigger the gesture. Movement follows distance and respects pause and reduced motion.

Review contact-sheet.png, pointing-glow-preview.webp and board-preview.png. Asset and browser validation reports are retained. Only Rogue's board mapping is activated; other roster identities remain independent.
